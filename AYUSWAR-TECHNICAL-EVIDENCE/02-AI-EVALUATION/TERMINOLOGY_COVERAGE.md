# Terminology match coverage (RxNorm)

Measured: of the medication terms the deterministic fallback extractor emits
over the synthetic clinical fixtures, how many resolve exactly in the public
NLM RxNorm REST API. Machine-readable data: [TERMINOLOGY_COVERAGE.json](TERMINOLOGY_COVERAGE.json),
generated by `scripts/measure_terminology_coverage.mjs` (live API queries;
fails loudly when RxNorm is unreachable).

## What this is NOT

This is not entity precision or recall. There is no gold-standard annotation
of the fixtures and no error-analysis denominator, so a per-entity accuracy
number cannot be computed and is not claimed. A resolved term means the
string exists in RxNorm, not that the mention is clinically correct in
context.

## Result (run recorded in the JSON)

- Medication terms emitted by the fallback extractor: 2
  (`paracetamol`, `penicillin` - the latter normalized from the Telugu
  `పెన్సిలిన్` via the extractor lexicon map).
- Exact RxNorm resolution: 1 of 2 (coverage 0.5).
  - `paracetamol` exact-resolves to RxCUI 161 (the acetaminophen
    ingredient).
  - `penicillin` does not exact-resolve; RxNorm's approximate matcher
    returns only a generic `PENICILLIN` candidate (RxCUI 7986). RxNorm is
    US-centric and specific penicillin ingredients (penicillin V,
    amoxicillin, ...) live under their own ingredient names.
- Symptoms and allergy entities extracted in the same run (4 SYMPTOM, 1
  ALLERGY) are out of scope for a drug terminology and were not looked up.
  Resolving those would need a clinical terminology such as SNOMED CT or
  LOINC; SNOMED CT requires a license/account and was not used.

## Caveats

- RxNorm covers US drug terminology with English names; Indian-market brand
  names and formulations are under-represented.
- Telugu-script drug forms reach RxNorm only through the extractor's
  lexicon normalization map; unmapped transliterations fail closed (no
  match, no guess), consistent with the extractor's documented
  citation-form-only scope.
- Synthetic fixtures and public drug names only; no patient data was sent
  to the external API.
